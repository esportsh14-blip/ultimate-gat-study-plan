Good to know

Questions, answered.

Which plan should I choose?

If your test is under 5 weeks away, take the Sprint. Around 6–7 weeks, the Momentum. Two months or starting fresh, the Foundation.

Do I need a strong base?

No. The 60-day Foundation starts from zero. The 30-day Sprint assumes you already know the basics and want to peak.

Is this for القدرات?

Yes — these plans target the General Aptitude Test (GAT / القدرات العامة), covering both the Verbal and Quantitative sections.
